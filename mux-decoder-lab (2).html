Notes

Input format and assumptions

  • Variables are A (most significant bit) through D. Minterm m is the binary number ABCD read as an integer.
  • Supported sizes: 2, 3 or 4 variables. Minterms must be whole numbers from 0 to 2ⁿ − 1.
  • Separators: commas, spaces or semicolons. Forms like m5 and Σm(1,2) are accepted.
  • Duplicates are merged with a warning. An empty list means F = 0.
  • Don't-cares are not modelled. Every unlisted minterm is 0.
  • Faults are single stuck-at faults on the physical line, one at a time.

How each realization works

  • 2ⁿ:1 MUX. All n variables drive the selects. Tie input Iₘ to F(m).
  • 2ⁿ⁻¹:1 MUX. Choose one variable X as data. For each select value, look at the minterm pair (X = 0, X = 1): (0,0) gives 0, (1,1) gives 1, (0,1) gives X, (1,0) gives X′.
  • Decoder + OR. The decoder raises exactly one output Dₘ. OR together the outputs for the minterms of F.
  • Decoder + NAND. With active-low outputs Yₘ, a NAND of the chosen lines gives the same OR.
